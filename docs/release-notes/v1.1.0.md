## Cycling v1.1.0 — pause step counting

**Update:** install `Cycling-v1.1.0.apk` over v1.0.0. Your steps, rides and calibration are kept
(same signing key; the database is upgraded automatically from schema 1 to 2).

### New
- **Pause step counting** for motorbike, car or bus journeys, where vibration and bumps can register as steps.
  Choose 30 min, 1 h, 2 h or "until I resume". Available on the Today screen and as a **Pause steps 1 h** button
  in the tracking notification; **Resume steps** is in the notification while paused.
- Timed pauses resume automatically. Steps the sensor registers during a pause are never added.
- The pause survives an app restart or phone reboot.

### Changed
- Database schema v2 (adds one column), with a tested migration that keeps all v1.0.0 data.

### Verification status
- 55 automated tests passing (new: pause rules, pause end-to-end, v1→v2 migration); Android Lint passes.
- Not yet tested on a physical phone.

### Known limitation
- When the phone is in deep sleep, a timed pause may resume a few minutes late (when the phone next wakes);
  a few real steps in that window may not be counted.
