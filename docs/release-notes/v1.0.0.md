## Cycling v1.0.0 — first release

**Install:** download `Cycling-v1.0.0.apk` below on your phone, open it, allow "Install unknown apps" for your
browser/Files app, and tap Install. Requires Android 10 or newer.

### Features
- Background walking tracker, on by default after onboarding (hardware step counter; accelerometer fallback).
- GPS cycling rides with Pause/Resume, Finish and Discard; walking is paused automatically during rides.
- Tracking Mode Controller: walking and cycling never count the same movement; safe recovery after app restart or reboot.
- Calibration Centre: step length (single-step median, measured course, manual; walking and running) and
  1 km GPS distance calibration with quality checks, explicit confirmation and reset.
- Weekly analysis, all-time totals, ride history with route drawing and raw-vs-corrected distance.
- Eight themes with vector artwork. Release APK ≈ 2.9 MB. No internet permission; data stays on the phone.

### Verification status
- Built and signed (APK Signature Scheme v2), 47 automated unit/integration tests passing, Android Lint clean of errors.
- **Not yet tested on a physical phone** — see `docs/TESTING.md` for the on-phone acceptance checklist.

### Known limitations
- Steps taken while a ride is active are not counted (by design).
- A ride interrupted by a phone restart must be resumed manually; the distance during the gap is not recorded.
- Calories are estimates.
